Inventory Reservation System

Purchase flow

1. Customer clicks Buy

The service checks whether the requested quantity is available.

2. Stock is reserved

If available, an order becomes RESERVED. Its units are held for 2 minutes by default while the customer pays.

Payment succeedsCONFIRMEDHeld stock becomes sold stock.
Cancel / payment failsCANCELLED / PAYMENT_FAILEDHeld stock becomes available again.
Unpaid at the deadlineEXPIREDThe expiry worker releases the held stock.
